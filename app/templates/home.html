THE PRODUCT

One operating system for inbound revenue recovery.

Not a chatbot. A production workflow that changes business state and records the evidence.

01

Voice intake that takes action

Route intent, answer approved questions, qualify service demand, and trigger the correct workflow from a real phone call.

02

Grounded retrieval

Use tenant-approved knowledge, citations, retrieval thresholds, and human escalation when evidence is weak.

03

Reliable workflow writes

Book appointments, upsert HubSpot contacts, and send Twilio confirmations without duplicate downstream actions.

04

Outcome-first analytics

Track call volume, booking conversion, recovered revenue, cost per booking, CRM completion, SMS delivery, latency, and escalation rate from persisted traces.

THE VERTICAL SLICE

Every step is inspectable. Every outcome is verifiable.

01

Call arrives
