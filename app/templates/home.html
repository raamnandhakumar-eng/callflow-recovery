Turn a caller's words into a business action.

CallFlow Recovery takes two inputs: the phone number the customer is calling from and the call transcript. It figures out what the customer needs, answers from approved business knowledge, creates an appointment when appropriate, escalates risky or unsupported requests, and records the full outcome.

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU SUBMIT

The transcript determines the workflow.

No scenario selector is required. CallFlow interprets the caller's words and chooses the path.

01

Understand

Detect the caller's intent and extract useful details such as the requested service and time.

02

Check knowledge

Retrieve approved company information before generating a customer-facing answer.

03

Take action

Create an appointment, answer the question, or escalate the request based on the transcript.

04

Record the outcome

Persist the intent, action, citations, downstream adapter results, latency, and execution trace.

SAMPLE OUTCOMES

One input format, different actions.

The dashboard includes sample transcripts for each path, or you can type your own.

01

Appointment

“I need an AC diagnostic Thursday at 2 PM.” → detect booking intent, extract service/time, create the appointment, and record downstream actions.

02

Pricing

“How much is a diagnostic visit?” → retrieve approved pricing information and answer with a citation.

03

Emergency

“There is smoke coming from my furnace.” → identify a high-risk request and escalate rather than pretending to resolve it.

04

Unsupported

An unsupported question → escalate and record a knowledge gap for later human review.
