Four paths. One observable workflow.

01

Appointment

Creates an appointment record, updates the CRM adapter, produces an SMS adapter result, and persists the trace.

02

Pricing

Retrieves approved service policy, generates a grounded answer, and stores citations used for the response.

03

Emergency

Routes high-risk requests to escalation instead of fabricating a customer-facing resolution.

04

Knowledge gap

EXECUTION MODEL

Every customer-facing claim maps to an inspectable step.

01

Route intent

A lightweight classifier chooses appointment, FAQ, emergency, reschedule, or unsupported paths.

02

Retrieve approved knowledge

Tenant documents provide grounded context and citations. Weak evidence triggers escalation.

03

Change business state

The workflow creates the appointment before claiming success and protects downstream actions from replay duplication.

04

Persist evidence

Outcome, latency, cost, citations, integration IDs, and execution steps are stored for review.

DEPLOYMENT BOUNDARY

A working public demo with a strict path to live integrations.

The public deployment runs in explicit demo mode so anyone can test it without credentials. CRM and SMS use deterministic simulated adapters when credentials are absent. Setting DEMO_MODE=false makes those integrations fail closed unless live credentials are configured.
